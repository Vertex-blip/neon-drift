# Godot edition: art credits

The ten playable car meshes and gas station were sourced from the [carros open model library](https://github.com/pedrosoandre/carros), originally published by the named Sketchfab artists. Each selected asset is under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The library simplified and meshopt compressed the originals. For this game, the geometry was decompressed for Godot, embedded WebP textures were converted to PNG where needed, and the models were scaled, positioned and given selectable paint materials at runtime. The original authors retain ownership of their work.
